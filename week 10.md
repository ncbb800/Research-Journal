# Research-Journal
Research Journal for AI Level 2 Research Course

### This Week's Method
(What research method did we learn in class? e.g., comparative analysis, adversarial testing)

Comparative Evaluation + Human-Centered Analysis

### How I Applied It
This week focused on how humans perceive image quality compared to technical performance.

Models tested:

playgroundai/playground-v2.5-1024px-aesthetic
RunDiffusion/Juggernaut-XI-v11
FLUX.1-dev
Animagine-XL-3.1

Generated image sets across:

Portraits
Landscapes
Stylized art
Fantasy scenes

Asked several classmates/friends to compare outputs without knowing which model generated them.

Evaluation criteria:

Realism
Creativity
Emotional impact
Overall preference

### What I Expected
(My prediction before testing)

Technically advanced models would consistently receive the highest ratings
Photorealistic outputs would be preferred overall
Anime/stylized models would perform best only in niche categories

### What I Found
(Key observations — what happened?)

Human preference did not always match technical realism
Playground v2.5 was often preferred because of its cinematic aesthetic style
Juggernaut received strong realism scores but was sometimes considered “less interesting”
Animagine-XL performed very well in emotional expression and stylized categories
Some imperfect images were preferred because they appeared more creative or expressive

### Why I Think This Happened
(My explanation — connect it to training data, model design, domain, etc.)

Human evaluation of art is subjective and influenced by:

Style
Emotional resonance
Composition
Familiarity

Technical image quality alone does not determine perceived value.
This suggests AI image systems should optimize not only for realism, but also for aesthetics and user preference.

### Limitations
(What couldn't I test? What might be different with other data/models/topics?)

Small and informal evaluation group
No formal scoring rubric
Preferences may vary across cultures or artistic backgrounds

### What I Want to Try Next
(Where is my investigation going? What question am I circling?)

Design a more systematic evaluation framework
Compare human ratings against automated image metrics
Investigate personalization in AI image generation systems
